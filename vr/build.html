Build Your 3D Scene

One cube per line: x, y, z, size, color. x is left/right, y is up, z is forward/back (negative is in front of you). Lines that start with # are notes. Press Preview, drag the picture with the mouse to look around, then press Make my link and open the link on the phone.

Send the link to a grown-up's phone (text or email), or type it in. Open it on the S7, tap Start, then put the phone in the headset.
