Build Your 3D Scene

How it works
1. Every row below is one cube. Change the numbers or the color and watch the preview.
2. Press + Add a cube to make another one.
3. When you like it, press Make my link, then open that link on the phone and put the phone in the headset.
You always start in the middle of the world, looking straight ahead. Think of the numbers as steps from where you stand.

Advanced: type the cubes as text

One cube per line: x, y, z, size, color. Lines that start with # are notes. Press Use this text to turn the lines into cubes. If a line has a mistake, it tells you which one.

Preview

Drag with the mouse to look around. The two pictures are what your left and right eye will see. Use W A S D to walk, and Space to put a cube down.

Send the link to a grown-up's phone (text or email), or type it in. Open it on the S7, tap Start, then put the phone in the headset.
